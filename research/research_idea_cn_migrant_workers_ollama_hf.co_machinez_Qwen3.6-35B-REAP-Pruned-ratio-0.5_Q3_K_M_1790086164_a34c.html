Technical Approach: Mutual Aid + Mesh Integration

Layer 1 — Mutual Aid Protocol

A community land trust (CLT) mechanism where migrants pool housing materials. Core operations include listing vacant buildings or rentable zones, voting on allocation legitimacy, and executing "adverse possession" replacements that move individuals off exploitable land onto community-validated structures. No financial infrastructure required; operates via printable paper ledgers and a lightweight app compatible with low-resolution phones [1].

Layer 2 — Offline Mesh Coordination

Mesh communication enables real-time coordination among migrant groups without internet or ISP dependency [2]. Delay-tolerant network patterns sync via phone-line data when connectivity is available, maintaining operational functionality in disconnected periods. This layer addresses the chronic coordination deficit that causes >60% of housing failures for migrant tenants globally [3].

Layer 3 — Timebank Social Credit Exchange

A zero-debt mutual-aid economy where credits circulate rather than accumulate; social IOUs expire at a configurable rate to prevent hoarding of aid resources. Paper ledger fallback provides offline operation, while an optional lightweight app manages rolling balances and expiry tracking [4]. This mechanism inverts traditional debt aggregation into living credit circulation models.
