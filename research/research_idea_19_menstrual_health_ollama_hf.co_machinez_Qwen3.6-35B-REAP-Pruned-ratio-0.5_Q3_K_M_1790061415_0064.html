Open-Source Guide Framework

A publicly accessible open-source guide covering: (a) hygiene patterns adapted for sheltered environments without electricity; (b) low-cost pain management techniques; and (c) safe reuse protocols for menstrual products in shared care settings. This approach mirrors WHO guidelines on community health worker resources at scales where formal clinical infrastructure is absent [3].

Recommended content includes printable QR-code references, diagram-based no-shame instruction flows adapted from the oral knowledge pipeline used by village elders, and low-parameter sampling (0.1–0.3) to reduce hallucinated or unsafe advice when deployed on resource-constrained devices via lightweight LLMs [4].
